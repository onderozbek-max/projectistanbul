Prop legend: role grammar (R1-T4 part 11)

dressing sidewalk slot: flatter (x0.8 tall), smaller (x0.9), muted, faint outline
solid cover block: thick outline, top highlight, hatched top
breakable cover block: thick outline, lighter top, crack
hazard keeps its colours (fuel, fire, stripes)
fauna reacts to Arda: full colour, own silhouette
plain landmark, overhead rig, decal or fx: drawn as before

Each cell is drawn by the game's own painter (P.Props.draw, P.Vehicles.drawCar). The same kind appears as dressing and as a block where both exist, so the difference is visible. Blocks use the sim size from config-levels.js (P.CFG.levels.kinds). Open a phone-width window to check legibility at 390 px.
