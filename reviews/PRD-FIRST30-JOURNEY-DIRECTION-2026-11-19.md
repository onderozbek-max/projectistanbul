# First 30 levels journey and scene direction

Companion to the [production brief](PRD-FIRST30-EXPERIENCE-RESET-2026-11-19.md). This is proposed creative treatment of existing first30 mechanics, informed by Audit10 and owner feedback. It is not a new claim of manual completion or an authorization to expand beyond30.

## 1. The experience across a session

The journey needs changes in what the player does and feels, not constant peak intensity. Alternate pursuit, deliberate positioning, risky commitment, earned power, human interruption and release. Preserve short connective levels and quiet aftermaths. Novelty is not measured by adding one extra objective to every level.

For every level, the implementation ledger must state: the decision visible **before** commitment; the consequence caused by the player; the next action made possible; the emotional/pacing role; and remaining uncertainty in actual play. A renamed prop, a new illustration or a 3.8-second generic enemy diversion does not establish a different decision.

Review transitions as well as levels: loading → first controlled movement; combat → reward choice → return; boss → aftermath → comic → result; defeat → lesson → growth → retry; ending → honest slice boundary. Preserve heading/facing, camera, audio, touch state and player intention across each transition. A smooth clip of the central fight cannot certify the whole journey.

## 3. Five memorable moments to make unmistakable

These use existing systems; they are not five new modes or unrelated mechanics.

1. **The street answers the player:** L2 tram call, L8 bell lure and L24 trolley should show a readable chain from operation to crowd movement to usable escape. Keep the player in control of the decisive crossing. The visual payoff is the changed street, not a success toast.
2. **Turn the boss's environment against her:** L15's existing cart-created opening should let a player recognize a route, bait/position within the supported mechanic, see the collision affect rope/body, then exploit it. No fake counter animation when the actual counter did not occur.
3. **A found object becomes an intentional weapon or shelter:** L22 handcart/placed-cover alternatives should produce visibly different crowd/route outcomes. Carrying, releasing and impact share a physical source. Neutral remains valid; choosing offense is not secretly mandatory.
4. **Someone has your back:** L21 stages Kaan's actual assistance where player, companion and threat are readable together. L23 provides reciprocity. The player should remember what Kaan enabled, not just a portrait bubble thanking them.
5. **Power changes the next encounter:** an earned evolution arrives with enough meaningful combat left, transforms the actual action and produces a legible first use. The reveal prepares the player to notice changed behavior; it does not substitute for that behavior.

These are the game's distinctive appeal: Istanbul becomes usable space, the build becomes a style of action, and companionship becomes an experienced relationship. More bosses, weapons or currencies are not required to make these moments stronger.

## 4. Scene direction and handback

Existing `scene-flow.js` and `scene-acting.js` provide protected clocks, obstacle-aware staging and limited pose selection. Preserve those correctness repairs. Direct specific bodies and reactions on top; a path completed successfully does not prove acting.

Every scene sheet specifies entry condition, actor positions/attention, ordered action/reaction, source of sound, camera focus, what changes, first controlled action after handback, repeat/skip behavior and interruption cleanup. Tune durations through viewing; do not turn fixed seconds into acceptance proxies. Keep reading time independent of animation speed and preserve pause access.

### S01 Opening and first control

Retain current approved conversion and knife source. Make the sequence causally readable: stranger changes; Arda's attention and weight react; hand reaches the real knife; first threat demands movement; control returns in a facing/camera position consistent with that threat. No duplicate limbs, size pop, offscreen attacking or new manual mechanic that contradicts automatic combat. Keep the first controlled action early and understandable.

### S02 All ten boss entrances and defeats

Use each [boss's specified performance](PRD-FIRST30-COMBAT-CRAFT-2026-11-19.md). Entrance establishes its tool, bodily character and arena problem. Defeat resolves the actual attack, tool, body and space before modal rewards. A universal zoom/nameplate/bounce does not meet ten scene requirements. Reduced motion retains ordering and readable composition without shake/zoom. First view, short repeat and skip converge on the same valid game state.

### S03 L1 shopkeeper and aftermath

Show the person/shutter action creating shelter before recap. Arda's attention follows the actual invitation; return into a visible reachable route. Preserve current script and improved comic art. Avoid explaining the same action in a world caption, comic and result message. Earned progression follows a settled result, not a fourth overlay during the emotional beat.

### S04 L7 musician, L17 rescue and L27 guide

Use short live scenes where feasible. Person faces the relevant danger, follows a walkable route after assistance and visibly stops at a sensible destination. Gratitude/food follows the actual outcome. View turning and slowing at arrival, not just teleporting from work point to idle. Do not freeze the world for every tiny interaction or repeat identical grateful poses across all people.

### S05 L20 Kaan recognition

Fisher's body/tool settle first. Kaan holds a defensive fish posture and looks at Arda. Arda responds; that response precedes Kaan lowering the fish and relaxing. Both orient toward Eminönü, make space and take their first shared steps. Preserve the native introduction and agreement: Kaan checks the Captain, then accompanies Arda home. Pose changes must be connected, not eight unrelated stills. No Kaan assist before join and no premature permanent relationship grant before actual settlement.

### S06 L21 and L23 companionship in action

Stage threat → visible Kaan movement/intervention → usable opening for Arda. Later, Arda's assistance causes Kaan's work to resume and changes the passage. Keep source and beneficiary in frame, with ordinary control whenever safe. Only acknowledge help that actually happened. Distinguish offering an opportunity from forcing the player into escort babysitting. Avoid a giant “COMPANION HELPED” banner explaining an invisible action.

### S07 L25 Captain Nuri

Finish the anchor/chain/body action first. Kaan approaches along the real path, slows and stops at the body. Arda turns toward Kaan and leaves physical space; do not pose both toward the camera. Allow stillness and the specific memory without incidental barks, loud reward flashes or triumphant music. Conditional market-help acknowledgment requires the recorded help. Kaan begins departure and Arda accompanies him; preserve continuous positions and current protected reading. Grief is not a license to stand inside props or auto-consume resources.

### S08 L26 departure into Sirkeci

Lower intensity after grief. Service-car movement clears the actual passage; characters move through on foot. Ambient sound and body tempo can carry the transition without another page. Show forward purpose without abruptly cancelling the preceding emotion.

### S09 L30 service passage and call

Ticket Man settles; rail/signal motion resolves; passage becomes visibly usable. Both companions acknowledge it. Mom's call establishes Dad's knowledge/notes and the highway warning before the Grand Bazaar decision. Preserve the route motive and latest script. Camera/facing/control return into that passage; the slice ending remains honest. No random combat joke over the call or “next31” from an obsolete result branch.

## 5. Narrative and comic details

Preserve the causal route: dinner disruption → home for scooter/map → family at Grandpa's in Sarıyer → Paşa/bridge continuity → Kaan and Captain Nuri → loss → station route → highway warning/Bazaar. Do not restore superseded native scripts or create convenient new facts to justify animation.

Read TR and EN in actual trigger order, separately. Every observation requires its subject to be visible or previously established; every answer needs a heard/read question; a relationship line requires its actual event. Distinct voices and situational humor matter, but danger and grief need room. Repeated runs should use contextual novelty within existing writing, not automatically repeat the same surprised introduction.

Audit every first30 comic page/panel for: speaker/portrait match; gaze and action direction; continuity of clothes/tools/location; physically visible action; expression matching line; text at largest size; bubble order and occlusion; reveal versus intentionally blank future panels; tap/hold-skip; replay/back; missing-art recovery; page and panel audio distinction. Do not call an illustration complete merely because it loads. Preserve current ImageGen art and replace deficient specific assets through the established route when needed.

No more blocking pages to solve a staging issue. In-world action supplies cause and relationship; comics supply perspective, dialogue and story development. They should complement rather than repeat each other.

## 6. Environment, assets and composition

Give each district one dominant action landmark organizing the approach: İstiklal's tram street, Galata's vertical loads/bell, Karaköy's docks/cargo, the bridge's lines/wind/waves, Eminönü's market/quay and Sirkeci's rails/clock/service route. Use existing illustrated scenery and realistic walkable composition; more random prop density is not identity.

At phone scale, inspect tile seams/repetition, sprite scale, foreground occlusion, feet/shadows, prop pivots, break stages, legal hand position, carry layering and material contrast. Boss and hero must survive the busiest background as well as a clean lab. Illuminate or simplify the local encounter pocket without repainting every district into the same arena.

For each interactive asset: identify source illustration, state variants, real collision footprint, approach side, trigger reach, affected object/player and unavailable/empty state. Compare visible footprint with actual collision. Required doorway/invitation and real exit threshold must agree; optional props should not impersonate the exit. Preserve critical current artwork during loading/failure; do not reintroduce retired sprites into ordinary play.

Test shape/value differences on mute and reduced settings, smallest supported portrait layout, large text and left-handed controls. Decorative parallax/lighting never moves dangerous geometry relative to its hitbox.

## 7. Pacing and motivation review

Record where movement stops for a meaningful choice versus where it stops for mandatory UI. Merge or serialize interruptions so earned power does not feel like paperwork. Record travel with no threat, information, choice or atmosphere; remove repetition instead of multiplying rewards to conceal it.

After a win, a player should know what changed, what they earned and what they want to try next. After a loss, they should understand the actual cause, a plausible immediate adjustment and retained progress. Do not default to “get weapon to rank5” after a very early positional death. Avoid blameful coaching and monetization pressure.

Compare adjacent levels, whole five-level district arcs and the L20–30 relationship arc. Ask which decisions recur too often and where relief is missing. Retention is not established by a completed route: voluntary return, curiosity about the next encounter and remembered relationships need actual player observation. Finish the concrete production corrections before asking external research to explain obvious current failures.
