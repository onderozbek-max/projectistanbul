All 60 supplied power/policy cases clear; this establishes viability under those policies, not human fairness. Short rematches can reward good builds.

| Boss | Duration / observed families | Specific unfinished physical promise |

| Switchkeeper · L2 | 6.5 / 6.4; rails + hook | Hook hand, switch operation and rail consequence need separate body actions. Show a directional hook commitment and spent opening. Protect distinction from other worker-shaped bosses |

Every encounter needs a visible intention, an answer the player can choose and a consequence worth recognizing. Record close/ranged approaches, earned weak/typical/strong first encounters and rematches. Record starting position, actual attacks exposed, recovery reached, death source and useful reward. A higher win rate alone can mean either fairer access or less meaningful challenge; inspect why.

Do not make actors universally faster, add hidden counter-scaling, force invulnerable HP gates, or impose every pattern on every strong build. Fix missing or unreadable opportunities first. Source-specific anchors and timing matter more than a fixed atlas frame count.
