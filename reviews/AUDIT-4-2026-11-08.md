## 1. Verdict

**v0.4 is a lot of game, but nobody has tuned it, measured it on a phone, or made it safe yet.** Three things are measured
problems, not risks:

1. **Levels 2–100 are untuned.** Bot clear rates jump around with no curve: L33 8%, L47 25%, **L63 100%**, L88 8%. Inside
   one neighbourhood, stage 3 is harder than its boss (L23 50% vs L25 75%). Roster and theme decide difficulty, not the
   difficulty model. The "design oracle" can't be used for balance numbers yet (§3.1).
2. **The swarm fantasy disappears after Level 1.** Peak enemies alive is **94 in L1 but only 16–64 in generated levels**.
   The genre's core pleasure, mowing down a horde, is missing from 99 of 100 levels (§3.2).
3. **Audit #3's P0 safety list (its step 4, "before the next build") was skipped while the build shipped.** Scaffold trauma
   is still 0.4, the sledgehammer still draws ground cracks, there's no flash limiter, and the per-step enemy copy is still there (§2).

And one process finding, the most important in this audit:

4. **The engine gate (D-004) has been open all day while the "throwaway" prototype grew to ~15.7k lines.** It now has
   20 neighbourhoods, 22 outfits, 87 SFX, comics and a meta map. Nothing has run on an iPhone, Godot or browser.
   Numbers now live in three places that already disagree: the prototype config, the docs and the Godot core (D-025's
   XP exponent is 1.6 in the prototype and 1.4 in game-design §4.5 and `game/core`). Every extra prototype feature raises
   the porting cost and the drift.

**Recommendation: freeze prototype features now and run a one-week stabilization sprint** (§8): device tests, safety
fixes, a bot that models meta power, a balance pass on the slice (L1–30), one source of truth for numbers, then the
5-person "one more level" test. Only after that should more content go in, and preferably into Godot rather than JS.

**What's good and should stay:** the auto-attack with one thumb, the knife cold open, the 60 s hold, level-up and chest
spikes, the journey framing (D-027), comic busts (D-028), the dash/ult/frenzy skill layer, a deterministic sim with no
`Math.random` (verified), the bot and arsenal test harness, and an honest M0 packet.

**Findings**
- **B-1 (P0):** The bot has **no meta power** (Workshop, gear, skills), so everything past ~L30 can't be judged. Model an
  expected meta-power profile per level (from journey §6 and game-design §8) and apply it in the bot. Until then, balance
  claims beyond the slice are guesses.
- **B-2 (P0):** Even inside the slice, the curve isn't monotonic: L15/L20 bosses are easy, L23 (a route stage) is hard.
  Per-hood rosters (e.g. spitters, bloaters) swing difficulty far more than the HP curve. Normalize roster "threat budget" per stage.
- **B-3 (P1):** **L63 (H13 Samatya L3) is an outlier**: 100% no-death, 14 level-ups, 93 peak. Something in that hood's
  roster or theme is broken (likely an easy roster plus lots of XP). Investigate before tuning around it.
- **B-4 (P2):** L1 with the v0.4 kit is hitless for a kiting player (end HP 100 in 30/30). The D-020 "real threat" hold now
  only threatens naive play (straight policy dies 11/30). Fine for a tutorial level; re-check after the dash is taught (F-3).

### 3.2 The swarm is missing after Level 1

**B-5 (P0):** Peak enemies alive: **L1 = 94; generated route levels 32–64; bosses 16–24** (one outlier at 93).
The survivor-like promise and the M0 performance budget are both built around 300 on screen. The generator is spending
its difficulty budget on HP and specials instead of density. Recommendation: raise density and lower per-enemy HP
(same total threat, far more "horde" feel), and set a design target of **peak 120–200 on route levels from H02** and a
minimum swarm during boss fights (adds). Density is also exactly what the device test must measure.

### 3.3 Session shape
Median run lengths are 90–170 s (bot). With the map walk and comics, a hood is ~20 min. **Fits** the 8–15 min session
target in two sittings per hood.

- **J-1 (P1): H15 Zeytinburnu and H16 Kazlıçeşme are in the wrong order.** Kazlıçeşme sits right outside the Yedikule walls,
