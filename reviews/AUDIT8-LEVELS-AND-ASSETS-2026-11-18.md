All 30 world-action fixtures and campaign diagnostics were inspected. L1 has direct fresh-save play; later rows combine rendered fixtures, source and earned campaign evidence. The later hero-stride/civilian/opening-rule follow-up is reconciled in the main report. The level and asset strips below show the earlier frozen experiential build. Preserve the later authored strides and six civilian identities; re-check rescue/guide-specific acting instead of assuming that every living actor still uses the old single-person treatment.

**Execution priority after the owner's follow-up:** use [the revised implementation brief, section 0](AUDIT8-IMPLEMENTATION-BRIEF-2026-11-18.md#0-spend-effort-where-the-player-will-feel-it). Enemy-table directions below do not require a detailed new limb rig or new atlas for every ordinary zombie.

## 1. District identity and pacing

Keep the existing neighborhood route and mechanisms. Give each district a different spatial problem: İstiklal teaches street movement and improvised contact; Galata uses switchbacks and landings; Karaköy uses cargo and dock approaches; the bridge uses exposed sides and readable crossing intervals; Eminönü uses stalls, useful cover and cooperation; Sirkeci uses rail lanes and connected signals. Scenery should explain those differences before a caption does.

A useful rhythm is orientation → decision → pressure → exploitation/reward → release. It need not occur five times in the same order. Diagnostic “enemy within seven metres” time is high in many current runs; that is not a direct combat-intensity measurement, but it warrants checking whether quieter passages actually feel quiet. Do not refill successful fast routes merely to hit a duration target.

## 3. Ten boss directions

Durations below are diagnostic seconds from **typical earned-band, seed 3**, evasive/punish policies. They are not measured human targets. The L1 policy is currently identical in both runs. “Identity” requires seeing an intention, answering it and recognizing the outcome; it does not require artificially invulnerable phases.

| Boss | Diagnostic duration | Required physical and encounter treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Cook L1 | 14.42 / 14.42 | Distinct low carry, planted preparation, cleaver contact and recovery. Range determines close attack versus readable throw; show release from the hand. Preserve scale. Give the player a first learnable punish before a swarm/crowd hides him |
| Switchkeeper L2 | 12.93 / 5.85 | Hook hand and rail switch must be recognizable. Hook commits to a direction; rail lane changes the player's next position. Distinguish body/tool silhouette from Tower Keeper and Ticket Man |
| Roaster L3 | 31.45 / 8.52 | Heat/implement leads the roast; charge visibly loads weight before moving. Recoil/heat dissipation gives readable recovery. Cart/heat environment should explain the identity rather than a renamed large brute |

| Vatman L5 | 41.08 / 14.8 | Hand/shoulder actuates lever and bell; body braces against machinery. Preserve tram/bell/lever differentiation and improved punish access. Warn with physical direction while keeping escape in frame |
| Tower Keeper L10 | 32.42 / loss | Show counterweight suspension and a deliberate traversal threat. Fix the approach to recovery before increasing attack speed. The opening must be reachable by the melee build that correctly avoids the prior attack |

## 6. Enemy behavior and recognition

Sixteen enemy fixture identities include zehirli and kasap, which are additional cases rather than evidence that both naturally populate the first 30. **Screamer reaches an upgrade pause; the seven subsequent recordings are frozen and are rejected as animation evidence.** The table below is the required role direction, not a claim that those seven animations were visually verified. Preserve the natural-spawn inventory in the implementation packet; costume variants do not each need a new AI subsystem.

| Role group | Required contrast and adjacent check |
|---|---|
| Walker/diner variants | Uneven weight and unhurried reach; a turning civilian must transition convincingly. Stop reintroducing these as unfamiliar every level |
| Runner | Lower forward posture, longer stride, clear pursuit/commitment and overshoot/recovery. Faster sliding of the walker is insufficient |
| Crawler | Hand/shoulder-led low movement with continuous ground contact; avoid two body halves sliding independently |
| Brute | Broad planted anticipation and heavy commitment. Body should reveal the threat before a floor circle does |
| Hardhat | Readable protected face/head and clanking weight, distinct from ordinary walkers. Its reuse for Linekeeper currently weakens boss identity |
| Spitter/zehirli | Head/chest preparation, mouth/source, projectile/area landing and recovery. Essential projectile edge must survive foam/fire |
| Screamer | Head lift/chest expansion, audible/visible call source, then actual crowd response. Prioritize this high-impact role in visual composition |
| Bloater | Expansion/pressure cue and distinct burst posture before its true area event; death debris cannot impersonate a still-active warning |
| Kasap | Tool-led preparation and a committed cutting response distinct from brute contact; extra fixture coverage does not count as first-30 introduction |
| Parkur | Compress before the real jump/commitment, readable travel and landing recovery; no damage from an unshown destination |
| Çöpçü | Weight, carried implement and sweep direction explain its space-control role |
| Çaycı | Tray/throw source and trajectory read before the effect; keep it distinguishable from food/beneficial pickup symbols |
| Kurye | Vehicle lean, wheel-ground contact and braking/commitment; silhouette should warn faster than a label |
| Martı/pigeon | Clear flight height/shadow, targeted swoop and escape path. Ambient birds and stealing/attacking birds need different behavior cues |

Introduce dangerous combinations one relationship at a time: walker cover for a spitter, runner pressure during a heavy windup, then a controlled three-role combination. Escalate positioning and response demands before raw crowd count. An unfamiliar player should be able to explain which enemy they prioritized and why.
