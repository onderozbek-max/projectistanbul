## 1. Verdict (product lead)

**The machine works. The product isn't distinctive yet.**

The team ships fast and fixes what it's told: AUDIT-6's top ten is mostly fixed (§3), the bot now models meta power, the
sim is deterministic, and tests are green. But measured against the owner's bar (**differential value, hooks, wow, coming
back again and again**), the prototype today is a well-made **Istanbul-flavoured survivor-like**, not a game only Istanbul
could make:

| What the player spends time on | Where Istanbul is today | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| The play field (about 95% of screen time) | **Barely.** A grey cobblestone corridor, thin facade strips at the edges, a few props and signs | Screenshots `reviews/media/AUDIT-6/18-L2-horde-readability.png` and the QA screenshots: the "horde" frame shows 4 zombies at the top edge behind the HUD and an empty street |
| Mechanics (what you *do*) | **Almost nowhere.** Every hood plays the same: walk the corridor, auto-fight, pick cards | Istanbul wow moments W31–W57 (Tram Rush, halay line, pigeon flock, ferry horn, gull steals a simit, mannequins, turnstiles, fog off the Golden Horn) are **"proposed"** in `docs/wow-catalog.md`. In code the halay only exists in a bark line, mannequins and turnstiles only in story strings, pigeons only as ambient view (`js/view/istanbul.js`) |
| UI, comics, barks, names | **Strongly.** Signs (Kuruyemiş, Çay Ocağı, Baklava), TR-first humour, Mom's calls, comic pages, postcards | `strings-barks-*.js` (2,850 lines), `comic*.js`, `meta-collect.js` |

The genre core (auto-attack, cards, evolutions, chests) is **table stakes**: every competitor has it. The owner's
differentiators are: **the 17 km walk home through real neighbourhoods, the "No Signal" premise, Mom on the phone, deadpan
Arda, and Istanbul street life.** Right now those live in the menus and between levels. **The single biggest lever is to
move them into the play field, as mechanics and spectacle** (Tier 2–3).

Four measured problems block that, so they come first (Tier 0–1):

1. **The horde is missing.** Peak enemies alive: L1 = 92; generated levels **30–77** (§2). A survivor-like without a horde
   isn't one. (Raised in AUDIT-4 as B-5; still open.)
2. **The difficulty curve is still noise**, even with the meta loadout: L20 100% → L30 50% → L50 90% → L88 30% → L100 60%,
   and **L63 is 0/10** (it was 100% in v0.4; the hood is broken in some direction every build).
3. **Nothing has run on a phone.** REVIEW-M0 is still PARTIAL (T4 pending). The prototype has never been frame-timed in Safari.
4. **Retention can't be measured.** `js/view/telemetry.js` has no session, install-day or return events (AUDIT-5 F-1, still open).

And one process risk: the prototype **doubled in five hours** (15.7k → 31.1k lines). Each wave adds systems (attributes,
hacking, objectives, forks, mastery, events, affixes) faster than anyone can test whether they're fun. **Tier 0 includes a
system-count freeze** (no new systems until Tier 2's fun gate).

## 4. Findings by area (new in this audit)

### 4.1 Differential value and Istanbul authenticity
- **DV-1 (P0):** Hoods differ by palette, props and roster, not by **what you do**. A player who has seen İstiklal has seen the
  game. *Fix:* one **signature mechanic per hood** (a "hood verb") that changes movement or targeting, plus one
  **signature spectacle** per hood (T2-1, T2-2).
- **DV-2 (P1):** The title promises **"No Signal"**, but signal is a minor in-run event (`director.js:68` `signalLost`).
  The premise is the strongest hook the game has, and it isn't used as the meta spine. *Fix:* T4-1.
- **DV-3 (P1):** Mom is the emotional core but only interrupts (call pops). She should *pull* the player forward (T4-2).
- **DV-4 (P2):** The map is 20 identical check circles (QA screenshot), not the illustrated Istanbul D-027 promised.
  It's the screen players see between every run. *Fix:* T3-6.
- **DV-5 (P1, geography):** H15 Zeytinburnu and H16 Kazlıçeşme are in the wrong order. Marmaray station order is
  Kazlıçeşme → Zeytinburnu (`docs/journey.md` §2). Istanbul players will spot it (T0-6).

### 4.2 Core gameplay
- **G-1 (P0):** No horde after L1 (§2). Difficulty is spent on HP and specials, not density (T1-1).
- **G-2 (P0):** Curve noise and the L63 outlier (T1-2, T1-3).
- **G-3 (P1):** The idle player in the L1 restaurant gets nothing: after the 2 diners die, 0 enemies forever (measured, 300 s,
  3 seeds, AUDIT-4 Q-1). Route spawns are progress-gated, so a lost player gets no pressure and no hint (T1-4).
- **G-4 (P1):** **System overload for a casual mobile player.** In-run: 22+ weapons, forks at L3/L5, evolutions, dash,
  ult, frenzy, hacking, objectives, events, affixes. Meta: six attributes, mastery, gear, power lever, four currencies,
  Road Pass, daily, collections. Each is reasonable alone; together they are a lot of UI and rules before the player has
  learned to love the walk. *Fix:* an explicit **unlock ladder** (T1-5): every system has a first-seen level and a
  one-line teach.

### 4.3 Feel, animation and wow
- **F-1 (P0):** The **play field has no depth or light.** Night İstiklal should glow: string lights, shop windows, tram
  light, warm pools on cobbles, rain sheen. Today it's flat grey with dark edges. This is the biggest "immersive" gap and
  is cheap in 2D (additive light sprites, vignette, parallax facades) (T3-2).
- **F-2 (P1):** The hero is about 31 px and walkers about 27 px at 390 pt. Animation work can't be seen at that size (T3-1).
- **F-3 (P1):** Enemies spawn at the top edge **behind the HUD** (screenshot 18). Threat should enter from visible doors,
  side streets and tram stops, telegraphed (T1-1 AC).
- **F-4 (P1):** The animation set is uneven: weapon firing animations exist (`fire-anim-*.js`), but there's no
  documented checklist for hero/enemy states (idle, run, hit, attack wind-up, death variants, spawn-in). Without one,
  each wave invents its own (T3-3).
- **F-5 (P2):** Wow moments are generic juice (W01–W30 shipped); Istanbul spectacle (W31–W59) is unbuilt (T2-2).

### 4.4 Progression and return motivation
- **P-1 (P0):** Retention can't be measured (AUDIT-5 F-1, open). No session, install-day or return events (T0-5).
- **P-2 (P1):** Each hood takes about 20 minutes, but the reason to come back *tomorrow* is weak: no daily-changing street,
  no streak reward tied to Istanbul, no "next stop" tease on exit (T4-3, T4-4).
- **P-3 (P1):** Economy holes from AUDIT-4 still open: star chests at 10/20/30 per district are unreachable with 15 stars
  per hood (`game-design.md:326`) (T0-6).
- **P-4 (P2):** The map's end state shows "YOLCULUK BİTTİ. EVE VARDIN." above "OYNA · SEVİYE 5" (QA screenshot). A finished
  journey needs a real end state and a chapter-2 tease (T3-6).

### 4.5 Technical and process
- **T-1 (P1):** O(n²) separation and cluster targeting (§2). Fine at today's 30–77 enemies; **blocks G-1** (more density)
  on older iPhones (T0-3).
- **T-2 (P1):** View writes sim state (`coldopen.js:40`) (T0-2).
- **T-3 (P1):** The WAVE2-BRIEF tells agents the prototype is SoA; it isn't. Correct the brief so agents don't design on a
  false model (T0-7).
- **T-4 (P2):** Two string files at about 1,400 lines (T0-7).
- **T-5 (P1):** Docs drift: Godot XP 1.4 vs D-025's 1.6; PRD US-1.2 manual stab vs D-019; level-001 tuning predates
  D-020–D-025 (AUDIT-4 §7) (T0-6).
