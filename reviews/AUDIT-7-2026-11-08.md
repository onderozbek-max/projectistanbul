## 1. Verdict

**v0.7 is a big step up. It has real local charm, but it isn't distinctive yet, and five v0.7 features are broken underneath.**

What's good (keep it): local enemy types and objective framing; Arda's voice on cards ("Mangal için almıştım, işim değişti.");
evolution recipes shown on cards; an illustrated map; a death screen with the cause and a tip; a death beat; deterministic
replays; no soft-locks in 198 unlimited-HP runs over L2–L100 (code review).

What stops it from being *the* Istanbul game, measured on v0.7:

1. **The horde is missing.** In the browser at 390×844, on screen: **L7 4–11 enemies, L22 2–9, L33 6, L5 boss 2–5**. In the bot,
   generated levels peak at 30–77 alive vs 92 in L1. A survivor-like needs 100+ on screen at peaks.
2. **Hoods have local *skins* but not local *verbs*.** The same objective types and affixes repeat across hoods: "Tramvayı
   bekle" appears in Galata **and in Samatya, which has no tram**; rain appears in Galata and Eminönü. A player who has
   finished one hood has seen every mechanic.
3. **Five v0.7 systems are broken underneath** (code review, §2.4): the daily challenge modifiers do nothing; spawn entrances
   move set-piece enemies; a card/reroll race can apply the wrong card; settings can break startup; flawless counts are wiped
   on load.
4. **New enemies arrive with no onboarding.** Kasap at L63 takes the clear rate from 60% to 0%, and its wind-up has no
   sound or effect telegraph (§2.3).
5. **The screen is getting crowded.** HUD rows (HP, title, timer, XP, streak, objective) fill the top **~27%** of the
   screen. Cards carry about 6 lines of text each mid-fight.
6. **Still unmeasured:** no device run (REVIEW-M0 T4), no retention events (AUDIT-5 F-1).

Process risk: the prototype **doubled in five hours** (15.7k → 31.1k lines). Bugs #1 and #2 are what that speed produces.
**Tier 0 freezes new systems** until the Tier 2 fun gate.

## 4. Findings by area

### 4.1 Differential value
- **DV-1 (P0): local skins, shared verbs.** Objective and affix types repeat across hoods regardless of place. *Fix:* a
  signature verb and spectacle per hood (T2-1, T2-2), and objective framing that is **plausible for the place** (T1-7).
- **DV-2 (P1): "No Signal" is the title but not the meta.** Signal is a small in-run event (`sim/director.js:68`) (T4-1).
- **DV-3 (P1): Mom interrupts instead of pulling the player on** (T4-2).
- **DV-4 (P1): the 17 km payoff is a text line.** Reaching home is the biggest emotional beat of Chapter 1 (T3-6).
- **DV-5 (P1): authenticity slips Istanbul players will notice:** a tram in Samatya; the H15/H16 order (T0-6, T1-7).

### 4.2 Core gameplay
- **G-1 (P0): no horde** (§2.2) (T1-1).
- **G-2 (P0): new-enemy introductions spike difficulty** (L63) (T1-3), and the curve is off band at L15, L20, L30, L50, L88 and L98 (T1-2).
- **G-3 (P2):** L1 idle: the hint exists, but the timer runs with no threat (T1-4).
- **G-4 (P1): system load.** In-run: weapons, forks, evolutions, dash, ult, streak/frenzy, hacking, objectives, events,
  affixes. Meta: attributes, mastery, gear, power, currencies, Road Pass, daily, collections. It needs an unlock ladder (T1-5).

### 4.3 Visual, UI and animation
- **V-1 (P1): the HUD fills ~27% of the screen** (T3-7).
- **V-2 (P1): cards are text-heavy; the build strip is clipped** (T3-8).
- **V-3 (P2): banner collisions** (İKİNCİ NEFES over the objective bar) (T0-8).
- **V-4 (P1): the first room is greybox-grade** (`v07-L01-t040`). The first 30 seconds set the quality bar (T3-9).
- **V-5 (P2): the death-cause portrait doesn't match the killer** (T0-8).
- **F-1 (P1): light exists at the edges, the centre is flat** (T3-2). **F-2 (P1):** hero about 30 pt (T3-1).
- **F-3 (P1): telegraph events with no view** (CR-11) (T1-6). **F-4 (P1): no animation-state checklist** (T3-3).

### 4.4 Progression and retention
- **P-1 (P0): retention is unmeasurable** (T0-5). **P-2 (P0): the daily challenge is cosmetic** (CR-1) (T0-10).
- **P-3 (P1): unreachable rewards** (CR-8; star chests 10/20/30 at 15 stars per hood) (T0-6, T0-10).
- **P-4 (P1): weak reasons to return tomorrow** once the daily is fixed: no streak, no next-stop tease (T4-3, T4-4).

### 4.5 Technical and process
- **T-1 (P1):** O(n²) separation blocks G-1 on older phones (CR-6) (T0-3). **T-2:** views write sim (CR-9) (T0-2).
- **T-3:** the WAVE2-BRIEF SoA claim is false (T0-7). **T-4:** the bot is unreliable on defend levels (CR-7) (T0-11).
- **T-5:** docs drift (T0-6). **T-6:** system freeze (T0-9).
