## 1. Verdict

v0.4 has the right skeleton. It has 20 hoods in data (`journey.js`), a comic system with 26 pages in code (20 of them hood pages), 7 enemy types, a boss plan per hood, and a telemetry hook. Its problem is **variety and proof**. Hood 1 uses four enemy types, the scene and look are thin per hood, and there is no measured retention at all. The owner's six points share one cause: the content is generic, and the systems that would show whether it works are not built yet.

Three things should happen before more content goes in:

1. **Make retention measurable** (F-1, F-6). Without it, no v0.5 playtest can answer "does this bring players back".
2. **Pick the power lever** (F-2). Under the E2 enemy curve, power falls below enemy HP from about L60, and the proposed meta stack does not close the gap.
3. **Cut production scope to a first cut** (F-7). Start with six forked weapons, not fifteen, and one landmark set per hood reused across stages.

**What is good and should stay:** the hood-by-hood structure, the stage template, the knife grab in the cold open, the rising hold curve, the readability rules, the bot, the diner-turns surprise (W50), and the Arda deadpan tone.

### 3.1 Retention and measurement

| # | Finding | Pri | Recommendation |
