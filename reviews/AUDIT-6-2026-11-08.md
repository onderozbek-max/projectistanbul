## 1. Top 10 player pain points

1. **Start can die silently (WIP).** In the working tree, `ui.js` (WIP:17) maps `#streak` and `showHud(false)` (WIP:30) touches `el.streak.classList`. `index.html` has no `#streak`, so `playLevel` throws before the state changes. The title button does nothing and the screen goes blank. HEAD does not have this. Evidence: `13-wip-build-start-button-dead-blank.png`. Lesson: one missing DOM id should not block the run, and nothing tells the player.

2. **Dash and Nazar buttons paint over every overlay.** `.skills` is `z-index:6` (`css/proto.css:272`) and `.screen` has no z-index (`proto.css:59`). On level-up cards, pause, settings, chest and the down screen, the ATIL chip and the eye button sit on card text, the ad-reroll button and "PES ET". Evidence: `03`, `05b`, `08`, `14`, `15`, `16`.

3. **Story and call pops cover the play field and the decisions.** `.cm-call` sits at `top:150px`, `z-index:55` (`css/comic.css:35`). Mom's call covered the Vatman boss bar and the boss in the L5 fight (`04`, `07`), and sat over level-up card titles (`08`). Arda's bark bubble covers about a third of the play field at L2 (`11`).

4. **The hero can walk out of the picture.** Generated levels pin the camera at x = 0 (`js/view/fx.js:7`). The view is 10 m wide, so only |x| ≤ 5 m is drawn. The walk clamp is `corridor.xMax − radius = 6.6 m` (`js/sim/world.js:63`, `config.js:108`). At x = 6.6 the hero is not drawn at all (`12-L2-hero-at-right-wall-offscreen.png`). Enemies still path to him there, so the player takes hits they cannot see.

5. **Hero and crowd are small on a phone.** At 390 pt wide with 10 m across, the hero is about 31 px and walkers about 27 px (radii 0.4 and 0.35 m). In the L2 horde, enemies at the top edge are about 15–20 px. Blood and XP gems read better than the enemies (`18-L2-horde-readability.png`, `02`).

6. **Boss readability and fight length.** The boss bar is a 12 px canvas strip at `js/view/enemies-fx.js:202` (y = 132). It sits directly under the XP bar and reads as a second XP bar. The boss name is a tiny label. Vatman has 1452 HP. Knife-only does 14 damage per 0.75 s, about 19 DPS, or roughly 78 s of perfect hits. In my fight, 1452 fell to 1253 in about 18 s while the boss took the hero from 136 to 61 HP (`05`, `06`, `07`, `19`).

7. **Every run opens with the same three cards.** `js/sim/progression.js:116` checks `s.cardPicks === 0`, which is true at the start of every run. So the scripted trio (`w:nailgun`, `w:knife`, `p:shoes`, reroll disabled) appears on L2, L5 and every other start (`05`, `05b`). Variety is also thin: six weapons open at L1, the boss roster is three archetypes (brute in 13 of 20 hoods), and six base enemy types cover all 20 hoods.

8. **Arenas feel empty and alike.** All 100 levels use the same straight corridor (`config.js:108` into `js/sim/levels.js:25-40`). Hoods differ only by palette and landmarks. Side blocks are random AABBs (`levels.js:73-90`, 13 kinds, all static). The middle of the street is blank cobblestone (`09`, `18`). Bot medians: 1–2 level events per generated run (8 in L1), and 16–24 enemies alive at once on boss levels versus 50–59 on route levels. Interaction is limited to static cover, two hazards and the L1 cart and canister set. There are no weapon-by-terrain rules (no water, oil or electricity tags anywhere in `js/sim`).

9. **Run end and story friction.** Death cuts straight to the revive card with no death beat. The revive labels wrap ("REKLAM · PROTOTİP: ÜCRETSİZ" splits over two lines) and "PES ET (0 JETON KALSIN)" is awkward (`16`). A boss kill gives a 1.6 s "Seviye tamam" toast and nothing more (`09`, `main.js:246`). The end comic takes several taps or a 1.6 s hold to clear. Its single-panel page leaves about half the screen empty (`10`), and Turkish words break mid-word in bubbles ("KUZEYDEK / İ").

10. **Polish and guardrail gaps.** The pause menu ships the dev button "TELEMETRİYİ İNDİR (JSONL)" to players (`index.html:116`, `14`). Settings has no music, haptics, joystick side or shake toggle (`15`; nothing in `js/` calls `vibrate`). The hero's invulnerability blink alternates alpha every 70 ms, about 7 Hz (`js/view/render.js:41`), which breaks the 3 flashes/s guardrail. The dash label "ATIL" is not an obvious action (`js/strings-journey.js:7`).
