# Audit: game experience, design and product (2026-11-08)

**Trigger:** owner played prototype v0.1 on device: "nothing feels on… everything is low quality…
ayran, tereyağ, çatal? … what are those things that keep coming down like rain?"
**Scope:** browser prototype v0.1 (Level 1), all docs in `docs/`, `design/`.
**Outcome:** the P0s marked ✅ below are fixed in **prototype v0.2** (this commit). The rest need
owner decisions or later milestones.

---

## 1. Verdict in one paragraph

v0.1 was correct against the spec, and the spec itself was the problem. The Level 1 build used
kitchen items with stat-only upgrades, so picking a card changed a number and nothing on screen.
Every actor was a coloured circle. The climax was a timer you could not lose. The cold open ran
70 s and taught a manual stab in a game where you never stab manually. The "rain" was 26 fleeing
civilians drawn as dots streaming down the corridor, plus tea glasses drawn as falling rectangles.
None of that is a polish problem. It is a fantasy problem: the player never felt dangerous, and
never felt in danger.

## 2. Findings, prioritized

### P0: breaks the core experience

| # | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| P0-1 | **Build layer is meaningless.** Fork, Tea Glass, Sneakers, Ayran and Butter were all thematic jokes with flat stat upgrades. No new verb, no visible change on pick. | ✅ Replaced with 4 distinct weapon verbs (arc / auto-aim pierce / lobbed AoE / orbit). Each passive changes behavior: flat armor, heal-on-pick plus regen-when-safe, a global cooldown cut, harder hits and shove. Cards show before→after stats. (D-017) |
| P0-2 | **Nothing reads at phone scale.** Circles for every actor, no animation, no hit or death language, flat grey ground. | ✅ Procedural sprite figures: walk cycles, 8 outfit/skin variants, zombies reach toward you, runners hunch, death collapse, door emerge. Gibs and blood follow the hit direction, crescent knife trails, nail tracers, canister arcs with target rings, blast flashes, scorch marks. Cobblestone night street with shopfronts, awnings and lamp light. Camera moved closer (D-021). |
| P0-3 | **First minute is slow and teaches the wrong thing.** The cold open ran 70 s against a 45 s rule, and its tap and swipe beats taught a manual stab. | ✅ About 28 s with no input beat. Arda grabs the cook's knife and the first strike lands on its own: "ŞAK!" (D-019) |
| P0-4 | **No threat.** Segment D was a timer; v0.1 peaked around 65 alive. | ✅ Hold ramps 4 → 7 → 10 spawns/s, peak 100. Bot: autopilot 30/30 clears, 26/30 without dying; naive straight-line 26/30 clears, 11/30 without dying. (D-020) |
| P0-5 | **UI felt like a web form.** Thin buttons, white cards, text-only icons. | ✅ Comic-ink UI: chunky outlined buttons that press down, wide stacked cards with SVG icons, type tags (weapon / gear), NEW badge, stat chips, slide-in and pick animations, slam titles. New key art. |
| P0-6 | **"Rain" artifact.** | ✅ 12 civilians in 4 groups, drawn as running people with pumping arms and a "!" scream. The tea glass is gone. |
| P0-7 | **Fork identity is in 10+ docs** (narrative, cold open, marketing, store copy). | ⚠️ Prototype switched to the Döner Knife. 29 doc references still say fork. Update them **after** the owner confirms D-017/D-018. |
| P0-8 | **The story sits around the play, not in it.** Calls and barks pause or overlay but never change what you do. | Open. Proposal: Mom's call bars should map to a real mechanic (e.g. signal bars = card reroll charges, or the call drops when the hold starts). Owner call. |

### P1: will hurt retention or production

| # | Finding | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| P1-1 | Enemy roster is 4 types, 3 of them "walk at you". No ranged unit, no elite, no telegraphed attacker in the first 10 levels. | Add a spitter (ranged, telegraphed) by L3 and an elite (bigger, armored, drops a chest) by L5. |
| P1-2 | Boss spec uses a DPS-race model with no patterns. | Bosses need 2–3 telegraphed attacks plus an add phase; HP tuned by bot sim, not by formula. |
| P1-3 | Monetization: the Monthly Card dominates other SKUs on value per lira. | Re-price, or make the Card a convenience SKU (not a power SKU). Business-plan math needs a second pass. |
| P1-4 | Metric definitions conflict (D1/D7 denominators differ between prd.md and business-plan.md). | Pick one telemetry definition table and reference it everywhere. |
| P1-5 | Levels 2–3 have no spec; Level 1 is over-specified by comparison. | Write L2/L3 at half the detail before M2. |
| P1-6 | There is no game-feel spec with numbers (hit-stop, shake, knockback per weapon). | The v0.2 prototype values are a start (config `feel`). Promote them to design-system §7. |
| P1-7 | Gear names collide with passive names (both "Sneakers"). | Fixed in the prototype (Running Shoes passive); rename gear in content-catalog. |
| P1-8 | Copy: "bakı bedava" should be "bakmak bedava". | Fix in business-plan/store copy. |
| P1-9 | Guardrail gaps: refugee imagery, live broadcast parody, stray-animal harm are not covered. | Add to narrative.md Writer Guardrails (no harm to the cat or strays, ever). |
| P1-10 | CMP / consent and ATT flow not designed (needed for ads in the EU and Turkey/KVKK). | Add to M3 scope. |
| P1-11 | Android/Turkey: Turkey is about 80% Android, while the plan is iOS-first. | Keep iOS first, but budget the Android port before any Turkish UA spend (already D-014). |

### P2: polish and later

- Audio is synthesized placeholder. It needs a real hit/kill/pickup palette, music stems and Turkish VO.
- No haptics in the prototype; design-system specifies them.
- 17 small contradictions between docs (e.g. camera width, XP curve wording, cold-open length). Fix them when the affected docs are next touched.
- Settings lack colour-blind and text-size options.
