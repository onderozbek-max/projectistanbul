# Audit #2: gameplay, design and product (prototype v0.2 → v0.3)

**Trigger:** owner review of v0.2: "starts to feel like a typical survivor.io game… should feel superior…
needs wow factors… the bike chain animation is pretty bad, shooting animations are pretty bad, weapons look
the same… make the scenes feel more local, Istanbul-like."
**Status:** every item marked ✅ ships in **prototype v0.3** (this commit). The rest are proposals.

---

## 1. Verdict

v0.2 fixed the *fantasy* (real weapons, real threat). It then landed squarely on the genre's average:
- every weapon spoke the same visual language (white shapes, small dots);
- there were no reward spikes between level-ups;
- the street could have been any city.

Survivor.io-likes win on **juice density**, meaning something satisfying happens every second, and on
**spikes**, meaning a few big moments per run. We had neither. Differentiation has to show on screen
in the first 30 seconds. "It's Istanbul" was only true in the docs.

## 2. Findings and fixes

### Gameplay

| # | Finding | Priority | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **No reward spikes.** The only highs were level-up cards, and with 11 per run they became chores. | P0 | ✅ **Elite plus Mini Chest** (D-022). It's a breather and a power beat. ✅ **Kill combos:** x-counter with a timer bar, plus milestone banners at 25/50/100/200 (SÜPER! MÜTHİŞ! EFSANE! DURDURULAMAZ!). |
| G2 | **Too many card interruptions:** 11 level-ups in 2.6 min (doc target 7–8). | P1 | ✅ XP curve exponent raised from 1.4 to 1.6 (D-025): 9 cards + 2 chests. Same power, fewer stops. |

### Design and feel
