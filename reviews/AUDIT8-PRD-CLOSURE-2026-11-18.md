## Accepted deviations and corrected assumptions

- L2 retains the previously accepted protected tram payoff after a live approach/call. Do not undo it to satisfy the appendix's broad “live” wording.
- Major aftermaths now start at factual boss defeat before the true exit/result, with the shared gameplay clock protected. This is a documented implementation choice. Repair composition without creating duplicate rewards or requiring the player to walk away before the emotional beat.
- L13's points and L16's rods are route mechanisms. The supplied L13 rail hazard probe combines geometry with a boss rail attack; it does not establish that ordinary L13 has that boss. L16 rods are not an independently damaging hazard. Do not invent fixes for a nonexistent overlap.
- Storm Shield's relevant defensive trigger is Nazar dodge, not the manual Dash. Copy and demonstrations must describe the actual effect.
- Timings in the PRD were tuning ranges. The 5.2/6.4/5.2-second world phases are not failures simply because their durations differ; the missing readable physical story is the failure.
