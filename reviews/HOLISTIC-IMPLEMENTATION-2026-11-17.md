## Deviations and scope

- First-30 authored approaches now use base lengths of 200 m in L2 and 230 m in L3, down from 250 m and 287.5 m before seed variation. Other districts keep their existing length tuning. The shorter geometry did not consistently shorten the simulated L2 clear.
- Authored props have a 24-second carry budget, 0.35-second placement, five seconds of solid cover, 3.2 seconds of crowd diversion and a 0.6-second Dash regrip delay. Holding a cart, bench and bin uses movement factors of 0.86, 0.80 and 0.94. Carry wear and time do not reset on pickup. Scoped placement reuses the original prop art and checks an open path.
- Sheltered escorts advance at 0.83× their ordinary rate. The L23 prop repayment uses the existing 4.2-second window and 2.6 m reach, followed by 1.35 seconds of temporary relief.
- Clear Grit rises from 20 to 28 practice and damage-derived Grit is removed. Boss Grit remains 30.
- Early build opportunities apply through L30 with three cards. At most one surfaced Medkit opportunity is provided below 43% HP. Chest rewards respect the chosen plan. Evolution prerequisites and ranks remain in force.
- The exposed-boss preference applies within 0.28 m of the nearest valid target, with a direct line check. A threat within 0.92 m keeps priority. Vatman gets 0.82 seconds of visible recovery after an unblocked charge. Wall stalls retain their 1.45-second duration. All relevant damage warnings retain their original minimum and end points.
- Nearby normal ambient heavy spawn limits apply on L13, L19 and L26 within 8, 7 and 8 m respectively. A recent contributor appears on defeat after dealing at least 15% of max health within eight seconds. The history holds up to 24 entries and resets on revive.
- No new engine, dependency, service, currency or authored content past L30 was introduced. The current work stays local. The project’s earlier art calibration and shared asset sources remain present.
