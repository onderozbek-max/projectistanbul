# Audit10 · owner play feedback and revised creative direction

This addendum records the owner's nine observations after Audit10. It changes the weighting and next priorities of the [main report](GAMER-AUDIT-PASS10-2026-11-19.md). It does not claim a new playthrough, listening session or product implementation. Audio annoyance and the owner's moment-to-moment impressions are owner-reported. Existing independently verified UI repairs remain valid.

**Revised assessment:** the game has received substantial technical work, but the intended visible leap in core gameplay has not been demonstrated. The owner's play experience is a failed acceptance result, not something to dismiss because a feature or test exists. Dialogue/comic improvements from the separate narrative work must not be used as evidence that combat iteration succeeded. Expansion remains on hold.

## Why the earlier iteration method underdelivered

The brief spread effort across too many systems and accepted proxies for experience: a pose-selection function for physical acting, a health cue for noticing injury, an inspectable panel for discovering progression, and an event trace for a memorable payoff. These establish capability and correctness. They do not establish the perceptual outcome.

The next implementation must begin with a complete, visibly different playable encounter. Use the existing source as a baseline; retain working code and assets. Do not deliver another broad checklist whose individual changes are imperceptible. New mechanics and number changes below are proposals to implement coherently under the owner's next brief, not silent changes authorized by this audit document.

## Nine concerns translated into player-visible contracts

|Concern|Required result and concrete creative direction|
|---|---|
|1. Cook disappears into the background|At ordinary phone scale, muted, the player identifies the boss immediately without searching for its health bar. Give Cook a distinct value/silhouette separation, grounded shadow and selective edge light; suppress competing background detail/decals in the encounter pocket. Protect the cleaver silhouette. Compare with a busy ordinary build, grayscale and reduced effects. Do not solve this only by adding another arrow or enlarging the HUD.|
|2. Boss movement and unsatisfying death|Cook needs a connected body performance: supporting foot, turning hips, shoulder lead, cleaver load, decisive strike, weight transfer and recovery. Death needs a short authored sequence: interruption of the actual attack, a readable final impact, physical loss of balance, tool settling and an earned quiet/reward beat. This is the spectacle/closure principle behind a fighting-game finisher, not a requirement for gore or a long repeated cinematic.|
|3. Usta's Cleaver floats independently|Choose and enforce the weapon fantasy. A wielded cleaver must be held, loaded, struck and recovered through Arda's body. If it is thrown, show a deliberate release, outgoing arc and credible return/catch. If current orbital damage cannot support either, redesign the attack mechanic and balance together. Do not merely draw a held blade while invisible orbiting damage continues elsewhere.|
|4. Hurt sound annoys; mute injury is missed|Treat the current feedback as failing despite existing code. At the actual hit, show a compact hero-local reaction and direction/contact cue; retain immediate control. Make continuing danger/critical state persist clearly without requiring a glance at the HUD. Use bounded edge treatment and shape changes; do not flash on every rapid hit. Keep damage/heal/block unmistakably different. Replace or remix the reported annoying cue after actual listening; control retrigger rate, variation, sharpness and music masking. Haptics may supplement, never replace, mute visual feedback.|
|5. Boss strategy and build power lack distinction|Each boss should ask a different tactical question through geometry, tempo and target access. Mobile approaches may favor speed; short safe windows may favor close burst; exposed distant targets may favor ranged/explosive tools. Every first-clear build still needs a reasonable route to victory. Preserve powerful rematches and avoid mandatory speed/perk/loadout gates. Tune attack sequence, access, crowd pressure, recovery and damage budget together; HP alone will not create strategy.|
|6. Hours of work produce little felt change|Evaluate before/after playable sequences, not file counts, test totals or screenshots of new assets. The implementation summary must say what the player now sees, does and feels differently, with an honest normal-speed comparison. If the visible result is small, call it small. Do not credit unrelated narrative improvements to combat work.|
|7. Attribute/skill progression is buried|Use the first genuinely earned rank as an explicit opportunity: result CTA names the ready attribute and actual benefit; it opens the correct attribute; applying it shows the changed value and carries into the next run. Explain perks at the first relevant eligibility/currency moment, with requirement, cost and effect in the same view. Show where to return later. Do not repeatedly force a menu or display a zero-point allocation screen. Clear information after discovery is not sufficient discoverability.|
|8. Weapon motion/impact lacks immersion|Audit moving attacks, not stationary firing alone: sprint, turn, reverse, dash, acquire a target behind, lose the target, strike a prop, miss and hit multiple enemies. Maintain grip/source, momentum, actual hit geometry and a coherent return. Distinguish light knife cuts, weighted chain tension, motorized saw resistance, blunt rebound and explosive release. Fix the weakest contacts before adding particles.|
|9. Cutscene potential is unrealized|Direct action and reaction as miniature scenes that change play. Someone acts, another person responds, the environment changes, and control returns into that consequence. Opening conversion, Cook entrance/death, Kaan recognition, reciprocal help, Captain farewell and station departure need explicit staging/timing. Preserve native script and canon; do not fill the gap with more dialogue boxes.|

## First implementation target: one complete L1 experience

Produce one uninterrupted normal-controls sequence from the opening into the street, through damage and healing, a meaningful weapon transformation, Cook's entrance/fight/defeat, the shopkeeper transition, rewards, earned progression and the next run. Existing reward-collision and UI fixes remain part of this sequence.

**Cook entrance:** briefly establish the encounter pocket and readable silhouette; show him physically take control of the space through his cleaver and stance. A restrained camera push and local background/value change can focus attention during protected staging. Restore sufficient dodge-space framing before danger resumes. Reduced motion preserves composition without the push. Do not suppress necessary attack information for cinematic framing.

**Cook exchange:** give him an authored short pursuit/reposition, a clear committed cleaver threat, a distinguishable follow-up and an exploitable recovery. Make moving close, baiting and escaping different decisions. Show source before floor warning. Do not add fake attacks or secretly change collision to fit the drawing. Verify an ordinary invested build and weaker entry; don't force an invulnerable demonstration if the player legitimately overwhelms him.

**Cleaver payoff:** earn it early enough in representative invested runs to use it meaningfully. Present the transformation alone, then show the first actual connected attack/affected enemy. Serialize queued chest/story announcements. Its animation should look like a more formidable expression of Arda's fighting, unless an explicitly designed throw/return fantasy is chosen.

**Defeat:** as an initial tuning proposal, allocate roughly1–2 seconds to the physical impact/fall/settle, followed by a brief release into loot/route consequence. Tune by viewing, not a hard minimum. Freeze or neutralize only the appropriate encounter-owned danger during protected aftermath; do not leave unrelated damage silently killing the hero. Avoid a universal zoom/flash explosion for every boss. Do not repeat an unskippable long finisher on rematches.

**Progression:** when a rank is actually ready, show “Strength ready” with the real before/after benefit and a direct action. After application, return to the next-run choice. Make the benefit truthful; a tiny numerical gain must not be advertised as a dramatic combat transformation. If early growth is too small to perceive, that is a separate balance decision, not a copywriting problem.

## Apply the established quality across the existing slice

After the L1 sequence demonstrates an unmistakable improvement, apply the standard to all ten bosses and the full weapon roster, with individual performances and tactical questions. Cook's aggressive cleaver, Bride's rope/cart geometry, Fisher's tension/reel timing, Captain's heavy anchor and Ticket Man's signal/punch should not be reskins of one timing loop. Captain's aftermath should resolve into grief and companionship, not a celebratory execution gag.

For Kaan recognition: he holds his defensive posture, sees Arda's response, lowers the fish, both adjust their stance, then choose and walk the same route. For the Captain: finish the body/tool action, let Kaan approach and stop, let Arda make space, allow stillness, then begin departure together. For Sirkeci: let the physical passage open, both acknowledge it, and return control into the route motivated by the call. Use the current repaired paths/protection as infrastructure; direct the performance on top of it.

Carry forward Audit10's all30 encounter-variety work. Differentiate existing spatial/timing decisions rather than adding more mandatory holds or more systems. Do not discard fixed Memories ownership, compact cards, native dialogue, current approved artwork or accepted Arda gait.

## Acceptance replaces implementation checkmarks

The comparison must make these outcomes apparent without a developer explaining them: identify Cook immediately; recognize his attack intent from his body; notice injury while muted without watching HP; perceive a weapon as caused by Arda; feel a specific payoff on boss death; find and understand earned progression; see a relationship change through action; name a different tactical answer for different bosses.

Use identical representative before/after conditions for comparison, plus ordinary earned runs to avoid supplied-showcase bias. Include weak/typical/strong builds, target reversals, dense effects, mute/reduced presentation, repeats and interrupted sequences. Watch continuous native-speed motion and listen on the actual phone/headphones. Keep the technical tests, but never treat them as replacements for these outcomes.

The owner has already supplied useful negative player evidence. Further unfamiliar-player research should test generality and uncover additional confusion; it must not be used to postpone correcting obvious owner-reported failures. No report can promise a masterpiece, but the next build can and should demonstrate a visible improvement in a complete sequence.
