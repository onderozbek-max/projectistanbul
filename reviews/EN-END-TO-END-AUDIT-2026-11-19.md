## English comedy and character voice

The revised direction uses original satire of rules, entitlement, customer service, work and Arda's own rationalizations. It keeps Istanbul's places, objects and relationships. The target shows inform comic mechanisms, not copied dialogue, recognizable catchphrases or science-fiction vocabulary imported into this setting.

Mom answers safety questions and gives useful instructions; food and the jacket are recurring expressions of care, not compulsory punchlines in every call. Dad's terse warnings remain distinct. ## Context and mechanic corrections

The audit distinguishes observations, intentions, warnings and completed outcomes. An upgrade appearing is not evidence that it has struck an enemy. A timed event ending is not proof that the street is empty. A repaired connection does not mean Mom answered.

- Early warnings now anticipate a spitter, bloater or screamer rather than describing an attack that has not occurred. The shopkeeper asks Arda to keep enemies back while opening the shutter, not enter through a still-closed door.
- The elite line does not invent a person emerging from a door or a particular piece of armor. Kaan is not declared trustworthy before he has acted. A musician does not receive a name Arda has not learned.
- The Karaköy hazard line no longer promises a bloater-to-gas-tank chain reaction unsupported by the current event wiring. The L13 midpoint reflects tram controls and rails instead of an invented car blockage.
- World-event lines distinguish barricades blocking the route from protective cover, fleeing civilians from attackers, and pier activity from a guaranteed ferry arrival. Foam discharge and electrical events do not prove a fire went out or Arda was hit.
- Terminal interruption does not automatically mean the device is jammed or has locked Arda out. Successful controls do not guarantee that a target was struck or a phone conversation resumed.
- Combat and card lines remove unearned hits, escapes, purchase histories and inaccurate behavior. Their remaining humor belongs to the item or situation rather than claiming an unseen outcome.

The English source check also exposed residual Turkish core set-piece wording that describes attacks before they occur. Those exact observations are recorded in the story editor's trigger section. This English pass preserves Turkish; the prior Turkish report should not be read as a guarantee that every possible contradiction was eliminated.
