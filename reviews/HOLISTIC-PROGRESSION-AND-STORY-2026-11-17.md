# First 30 Bölüms · progression, story and asset review

## From a run to the next build

| What the player sees | Real rule and example |
|---|---|
| Rank on an upgrade card | Rank belongs to this run. Choose a starter, invest, match the named passive, and take the evolution at rank 5. The first generated first-30 route offer gives a ranged and terrain alternative when their families are available. |
| Survival option | One available Medkit can be surfaced below 43% HP when a card slot is open. Taking it can give up an immediate rank or a partner passive. Chest rewards preserve an established focus while that plan is unfinished. |
| Evolution plan | Knife + Gloves → Cleaver. Nail Gun + Energy → Nailstorm. Speargun + Gloves → Netspear. Molotov + Energy → Fire Lake. Gas Canister + Jacket → Cluster. The game tracks a real evolved hit, separately from equipping at the exit. |
| Attributes | Practice is attribute-specific. A ready Strength rank applies to Strength directly. Grit rises from clears and boss wins. Receiving enemy damage adds zero Grit practice. Applying a Grit rank adds 1.5 percentage points of maximum health and 1 percentage point of damage reduction before other modifiers. |
| Perks | Eligibility comes from the required attribute rank and Street Cred. The Perks tab applies the actual heavy-attack rule to the equipped starter and explains why melee practice and heavy-attack bonuses can differ. |
| Mastery, gear and bond | Each persists outside a run. Gear is equipped, mastery is assigned to specific weapons, and helping Kaan is a relationship action. Earned campaigns consume their own attributes, perks and gear each level. |
| Paused and about to retry | The pause screen starts with the current objective, health, Resume and this-run weapons. The defeat card names the final hit and shows a named earlier source when it dealt at least 15% of max health within eight seconds. The opening retry shortcut begins directly in play. |

Three automated campaigns used actual saved results, accrued gear, attribute practice and eligible perks. They used free first-run Second Wind if earned by the L1 script. Generated trials used no revive. They demonstrate supported builds, specific outcomes and difficulty examples. They cannot establish uncoached comprehension or the best reward cadence.
