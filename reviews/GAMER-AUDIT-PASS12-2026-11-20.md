20 November 2026. **Not a masterpiece. Do not expand production beyond level 30 yet.** Audit11 made useful corrections, but it did not establish the intended boss, animation, emotional or replay experience. ## System-level findings

### A12-01 — First-entry boss encounters can disappear before becoming experiences (critical)

Fresh campaign, winning attempts only:

| Boss / level | Fight seconds | Recorded windups | Interpretation of this case |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Cook / 1 | 3.87 | throw, throw | No close-combo lesson; evolved ranged damage dominates. |
| Switchkeeper / 2 | 4.35 | rails, hook | Two action names inside four seconds do not prove two intelligible decisions. |
| Roaster / 3 | 9.40 | charge, roast, charge | More opportunity, still needs observed response/consequence. |
| Linekeeper / 4 | 3.38 | relay | Dies with ordinary Knife5; fixing evolution alone cannot fix this. |
| Vatman / 5 | 38.05 | ten windups, four families | Materially different duration from neighbours; investigate actual access/choices, not uniform HP. |
| Keeper / 10 | 5.43 | weights, charge | District peak has very little room to register. |
| Bride / 15 | 10.90 | carts, rope, carts | Counter can exist yet be unnecessary/unnoticed. |
| Fisher / 20 | 7.70 | cast, net | Previous attempt lost after 12.82s; do not hide that loss. |
| Captain / 25 | 18.78 | anchor/spice sequence | More interaction opportunity; not evidence that acting/emotion passes. |
| Ticket Man / 30 | 6.27 | punch, signal | Chapter climax is shorter than several ordinary tasks. |

This single policy does not establish population medians. It does falsify a blanket claim that earned first-entry power consistently preserves boss identity. **The main problem is the encounter's damage and decision budget**, not simply lack of more attacks. First-entry encounters need room for anticipation, response, useful recovery and an escalation. Strong rematches should still reward mastery. No hidden build scaling, minimum death timer, damage cap or required weapon lottery.

Crowd pressure is also competing with boss authorship: current config retains boss add rate 1.08 of stage rate and six introduction adds. Do not assume these exact counts are wrong universally. Test whether avoiding generic pursuers is doing more work than reading the boss. A boss should own its arena's rhythm.

### A12-02 — Most levels are framed as permission to continue (high)

29 of the 30 sampled entry screens show the recurring **ROAD BLOCKED · REQUIRED** structure. The exact work differs and many consequences are real, but the dominant invitation is still “do the chore to proceed.” L6–10 share paving and upward framing; L11–15 share another; L21–25 another. Different props do not automatically create different spatial decisions.

Change selected encounters into choices about **how** to cross, redirect pressure, exploit terrain or help someone. Keep necessary story gates, but let their world state communicate necessity. Make landmarks and usable space dominate the composition. Do not add a second chore to compensate for the first. Preserve short relief levels.

### A12-03 — The payoff is frequently a screen covering the payoff (high)

Supplied L1 boss encounter sheet shows a large Cleaver reveal occupying the center. L10's encounter sample is dominated by the Evil-Eye graphic. L4's title occupies the attack area. Live L1 mixed Cook arrival with a Screamer tutorial bubble, then an evolution choice and another level-up during the encounter. These are observed composition/scheduling conflicts, not proof of permanent overlays.

The game must give a significant action exclusive attention briefly. Use compact reveal treatment, safe decision boundaries, actual weapon first-use emphasis and boss-specific staging. Do not fix attention by freezing every few seconds or withholding earned choices until after the boss is dead.

### A12-04 — Replay intention is weaker than the content catalogue (high; new design opportunity)

Current arsenal offers mastery information and selectable active/ultimate abilities, but base-weapon cards are not starter selectors; meta loadout is fixed Knife. The independent broad policy used **25 Cleavers and one Nailstorm** across its 26 evolved attempts. That is evidence of this policy's path dependence, not player preference or proof that other weapons are weak. Combined with a fixed starter and short resetting runs, it is a plausible structural reason for repeated build identity.

Nightmare, Night Shift, mastery, memories and other replay systems already exist. The missing piece is an immediately understandable **different plan for my next run**, with attainable different opening play. Promote the previously deferred earned-starter and curated tactical replay proposals in a bounded way. Do not add more currencies, modes or compulsory daily chores.

### A12-05 — The dramatic scale is smaller than the UI scale (high)

At phone scale, boss bodies and companion acting occupy a small fraction of the view while large labels, circular controls and effects command attention. L20/25 aftermath sheets have meaningful actor placement, but distance limits face, hand and posture readability. A technically articulated elbow can be imperceptible at that scale.

Direct frame composition, pose silhouette, material edges, body/tool contact and purposeful negative space together. Protected scene framing can be closer; live combat must keep the safe route and incoming threat visible. Brightening the entire screen or universally enlarging everything would merely create new clutter. Do not impose agility on the Captain; his weight and effort should be as intentional as Arda's movement.

### A12-06 — Progression explains more, but its immediate payoff remains modest (medium/high)

Live result has a direct “Apply your earned Strength rank” button. Attributes explicitly explain persistent training, and perks explain attribute prerequisites, Street Cred and weapon applicability. **The old “buried with no hint” criticism is no longer accurate.** Preserve this progress.

However, the first offered permanent change is +1.5% damage, followed by a largely locked perk grid. The UI is intelligible but not especially evocative of a new play possibility. Surface the next attainable behavior, the applicable weapon and an honest demonstration. Keep exact small stat gains honest; do not exaggerate them into a visual transformation. Reduce repeated recap/confirmation overhead.

### A12-07 — Companionship is real but needs a memorable playable exchange (high)

Source contains real Kaan interventions and reciprocity; earlier PRDs already specify them. This is not an absent-system finding. The risk is conditional rarity plus small-scale presentation: the player may never consciously connect Kaan's movement to the opening they use. Stage the first post-join intervention unmistakably, then invite later voluntary use through body language and geometry. Do not turn him into another meter or fragile mandatory escort.

### A12-08 — Some ordinary interactions remain visually ambiguous (medium)

Live restaurant exit required lateral alignment under a frontage/arrow; later Arda was partly hidden under the shop canopy while finding its entrance. Both worked after correction: **not a softlock**. Make the walkable aperture and occlusion cutaway clearer. A pointer should point through an opening rather than hide it.

Live health changed conspicuously in the HUD during combat, but sparse tool frames cannot determine whether short injury/healing cues felt sufficient. Source and supplied render checks confirm directional injury, critical brackets, heal transfer and shield distinctions exist. Do not falsely call them absent. Their muted recognition, annoyance, occlusion and sustained fatigue still need actual presentation acceptance. The Phone “NO SIGNAL” beat showed an empty grey portrait box; use a deliberate device treatment instead of a missing-person-shaped placeholder if that is the intended no-portrait state.

### A12-09 — The validation loop still favours what is easy to count (high)

Implementation is increasingly candid about its limits. That is good. But normal-speed captures without someone perceiving them do not close animation, listening or emotion. A policy clearing 30 levels proves reachability; a filmed mechanic proves existence. Neither proves desire to return. The owner's repeated lack of visible improvement means these gates remain rejected, not “probably passed.”

## Retention judgment

**Unproven, with strong structural risks.** Rewards, readable recipes, place and relationships provide reasons to continue. Repeated blockage framing, fixed early build identity, short/forgettable climaxes, interruptions and small visible progression weaken them. No audit can promise perpetual play or commercial retention.

The intended return loop is: “I learned that boss; next time I'll bring this tool and use that part of the street; I also want to see what happens to these people.” Evaluate voluntary replay and next-session return with unfamiliar players after the concrete repairs. Report observed cohorts honestly; do not invent retention percentages from bots or five informal testers.

The next visible leap should be **a different encounter and a different possible plan**, supported by better animation and direction. The PRD turns that into all-ten-boss choreography, all30 level decisions, authored highlight sequences, build/replay choices, weapon/feedback requirements and a bounded verification handoff. It does not promise that an overnight implementation can be certified a masterpiece.

Additional source-backed distinction worth protecting: Çaycı's thrown tea creates a non-damaging slow, Martı steals loose XP without contact damage, and Çöpçü's rolling bins/crates displace zombies and leave slicks. These are already useful tactical differences, not missing enemies to invent. Their presentation should make those different stakes obvious; generic danger treatment and a crowded chase can conceal the variety that already exists. PRD C4 specifies the actual roles and readable targeting/response contract.
