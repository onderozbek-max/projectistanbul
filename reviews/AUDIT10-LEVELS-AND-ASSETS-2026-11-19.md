## Enemy roles, consumables and object clarity

Sampled active fixtures cover Walker, Diner, Runner, Crawler, Brute, Hardhat, Spitter, Screamer, Bloater, Parkur, Çöpçü, Çaycı, Kurye and Martı, plus supplied extra Kasap/Zehirli. The first14 are the natural first30 roster; don't count the extras as campaign variety.

Isolated silhouettes and behavior differ. The test remaining is recognition in a crowd: sprinting threat, ranged source, armored obstruction, imminent explosion and lingering toxic residue must be distinguishable before contact. Preserve first-sighting history and repeatable urgent warnings. Do not spend on detailed ordinary limb rigs while critical threats are hidden by friendly effects. Ambient birds versus attacking Martı also need clear source/behavior separation when they share the scene.

Healing, blocked damage, critical state and full-health saved food are implemented. Full-health simit remaining available is intended behavior, not the original bug by definition. The player still needs a comprehensible source → contact → actual HP gain, while zero gain uses the saved-food response. Inspect simultaneous healing/damage so a net HP change does not erase the benefit's explanation. Health-station hold, breakable proximity and carried-object use need their correct local verb; avoid sending remote optional instructions during a required action.

The dense default/reduced sheets remain a concern: multiple SCREAM labels and BOOM fills overlap nearby bodies and area boundaries. Protect the imminent hostile shape, reduce repeated cosmetic lettering, and judge the whole moving scene. More rings around every object would increase the problem.
