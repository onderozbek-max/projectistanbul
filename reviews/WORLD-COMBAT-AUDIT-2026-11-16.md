# Street readability and combat feedback · 16 November 2026

The earlier [player audit](PLAYER-SLICE-AUDIT-2026-11-16.md) covered the authored Normal run through Bölüm 30. It did not inspect Ataköy. Procedural assets and layout logic were still used in later neighborhoods and some early ones. This pass checks those shared systems across the campaign.

| Player report | What I found | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blobby trees over the sidewalk | Ataköy combined a repeated overhead canopy drawn from overlapping ellipses with street trees and park decorations. | Removed the repeating canopy. Grounded illustrated plane trees and palms at the verge with space from nearby cover. The district tree uses its world anchor. |
| A green-topped beige building in the lane | A generic guard booth was placed as route cover, using a large square footprint and old procedural paint. | Replaced it with an illustrated guard booth at the curb. Its smaller footprint leaves the center open and gives the adjacent terminal a separate, reachable spot. |
| The turquoise car looked tipped over | The vehicle renderer rotated angled side art to match a north/south footprint. | Parked road vehicles use ImageGen top views along the curb. The shallow apartment-wall projection in Ataköy also uses the existing illustrated façade. |
| Objects looked usable without showing what to do | First-session hints expired, and idle terminal rings were large enough to obscure neighboring assets. | A compact symbol stays on a live terminal, usable station, pickup or interactive prop. One nearby action appears in Turkish or English, with a full-size ring when the hero can use it. Fuses show the danger radius and time left. Used stations and terminals keep their art and retire the action. Distant coins use small icons in crowded scenes. |
| Breaking and exploding objects looked alike | Both used generic particles, and a booth or grill did not have a distinct response. | Tea furniture, simit carts and market stalls break into material-specific pieces. Volatile parked vehicles, grill and booth props use a fuse, local blast and combustion debris. Fruit, porcelain and glass carry different shapes and sounds. Fountains, planting beds and trees stay solid. Extinguishers vent foam. |
| Regular zombie deaths looked like the same fall | The older system had different offsets but applied the same timed collapse. | Six body reactions cover slices, shocks, heat, launches, heavy blows and water hits. Motion and contact accents vary by finishing hit. |
| Boss victories needed their own visual payoff | Most shared a generic puff and ring. A reward or upgrade could hide the finish. | Each district has an authored motion, accent and sound beat. The Bridge Fisherman is swept over in a tide arc; Sirkeci's Ticket Man crumples as tickets fan out; Ataköy's boss drops into paving fragments. İstiklal's midboss presets also have their own cues. The brief scene finishes before gameplay and reward overlays resume. |

**Verification.** Chrome rendered one sampled street scene in each of the 20 neighborhoods and all five Ataköy stages (24 views). In a separate browser fixture, all eight service illustrations rendered, a used ATM stayed anchored, the Ataköy booth ignited and was removed, and all 20 district boss scenes returned control without browser errors. Six regular zombie reactions were triggered from real simulated kill events. The weapon tests exercised 17 base weapons at ranks 1 and 5 in 34 crowded mixed builds, plus 18 solo evolutions. The placement test checked 297 generated layouts from Bölüm 2 to 100: 182 booth placements, 87 parked cars, 232 healing stations and a walkable route on every seed tested. The Bölüm 96 balance bot cleared 7/8 runs with the normal revive allowance. No run timed out or crashed. Only one hit the optional timed objective. In a complete browser playthrough with the expected late-game loadout and simulated steering, seed 42 cleared Bölüms 96 and 97, then died twice in 98. A direct browser check of 99 also died twice. These runs exposed the need for a separate pace and balance review; they are not proof that a human player cannot clear them. The targeted suites and commissioned source checksums passed.

**Limits.** These are render, layout, interaction and combat checks beyond the earlier end-to-end run through Bölüm 30. They do not replace start-to-finish playtests of Bölüms 31–100. The campaign still contains older generic scenery in some unreviewed placements. Actual iPhone performance and player preference are also open.

**Deviations (D-099).** Booth footprint 2.04 × 1.75 m at x ±4.18 m. A booth terminal is 1.55 m toward the lane. Parked vehicle outer edge at x ±5.18 m. Generated example cover: tea and carts 25 HP, produce and drums 30 HP, grills 24 HP, booths 48 HP. Parked generated cars always ignite on a hit, up from a 15% chance of being explosive. Grill and booth fuses are 1.1 and 1.6 s. Existing blast damage, radius and chest rewards are preserved. Boss finish takes about 1.5 wall seconds; reduced motion and reduced flashes are respected.
